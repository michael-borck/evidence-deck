How to use it

Deal it. Work it. Commit to three.

1

Write the proof brief first

Intended use, affected people, baseline, acceptance rule with the blast radius — before the build, not after the results.

2

Label every claim

Observed, illustrative, or not tested. A demo persuades; a log proves.

3

Defend the dossier at the gates

Prototype, pilot, production, continued operation — each gate has its own evidence.
